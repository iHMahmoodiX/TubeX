# Project

**Owner:** Hossein Mahmoodi, solo product designer, educator and creator with two channels (design/education, AI/productivity). Channel identities are verified at connection time (M2), never hard-coded.

**Goal:** a personal workbench that covers the useful creator workflows of TubeBuddy and vidIQ with a faster, Persian-friendly experience, to save recurring work and strengthen distribution of existing videos and products. No promise of views, rankings or income.

**Product promise:** from any relevant YouTube or Studio context, understand the situation, pick the next useful action, and finish it with minimal switching and typing.

## Jobs
1. Capture ideas with their source while watching or researching.
2. Prepare a publishable metadata package (title, description, tags, thumbnail) per channel.
3. Improve existing videos from real evidence.
4. Make bulk changes safely (dry run, backup, explicit commit, conditional restore).
5. Recover from any failure without losing work.

## Scope
- In: personal, unpacked Chrome and Edge on Windows; local-first; optional AI with the owner's own key; YouTube official APIs with least privilege.
- Out: SaaS, accounts, billing, team features, cloud database, marketing site, store publication, automatic posting, scraping private endpoints, paid dependencies without the owner's explicit choice.
